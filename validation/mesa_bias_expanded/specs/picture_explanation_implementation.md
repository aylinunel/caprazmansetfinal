# Picture explanation — illustrative implementation

**Process and metric.** Each structured candidate interpretation has an explicit visual-support score in `[0,1]`. For candidate `i`, the caption-guided choice utility is `visual_support_i + strength × caption_specificity × 1[i.id == caption_target]`; the highest-utility candidate is selected, with input order resolving ties. The primary metric is the binary caption-target choice indicator (1 when the selected candidate matches the supplied target, otherwise 0). `active` is true only when the caption changes the selected choice relative to the visual-only reference.

**Task and baseline.** This models a single reader choosing an interpretation from supplied candidate meanings, not an image-caption detector. At strength zero, the reference policy selects the candidate with maximum supplied visual support. It is a transparent reference rule, not a claim of perfect or universally rational reading.

**Necessary condition and examples.** The caption target must be among the image's supplied candidate interpretations to receive salience. The positive scenario supplies a plausible but visually weaker target; at strength `0.8`, it overtakes the visual-only leader and increases the metric. The boundary scenario supplies a caption target absent from the candidate set; no candidate receives caption salience, so the selected interpretation and primary metric equal the strength-zero reference.

**Assumptions and limits.** Candidate meanings, their visual support, and caption specificity are explicit hypothetical inputs; no pixels or natural language are analyzed. Specificity and strength are illustrative settings, not fitted effect sizes. The mechanism captures a neutral, potentially useful way captions can focus interpretation; it does not classify captions as errors or assess truth, intent, or harm. It is synthetic only, has no human validation, and omits reader, cultural, contextual, and visual complexity. Passing tests establish implementation behavior only, not empirical validity.
